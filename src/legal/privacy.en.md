This policy explains what personal data I process when you visit kovar95.dev or send me an inquiry, why I do it, and what your rights are. It follows the Serbian Personal Data Protection Act („Službeni glasnik RS”, no. 87/2018, “ZZPL”).

## Who processes your data

The controller is Stefan Kovarbašić, a private individual based in Belgrade, Serbia. For any question about your data, write to [support@kovar95.dev](mailto:support@kovar95.dev).

## What data I process

**When you send an inquiry through the form:** your name, email address, phone number and current website (if you enter them), the service you pick and your message. I receive the same data when you email me directly.

**When you ask for a compliance check of your site:** I only look at the site’s public pages, as any visitor would. I don’t log in, fill in forms or buy anything.

**When you visit the site:** Cloudflare, which serves the site, processes the technical data needed to deliver pages and protect against attacks, such as your IP address and browser details. For visit statistics I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and doesn’t follow you across sites. I only see totals, such as how many people opened a page.

**Form protection:** before an inquiry is sent, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that it isn’t coming from an automated program. It processes technical details about your browser and device for that check only.

## Why, and on what basis

- To reply to your inquiry and prepare a quote or a compliance report. The basis is steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 12(1)(2) ZZPL).
- To protect the site and the form from abuse and to see overall traffic. The basis is legitimate interest (Article 12(1)(6) ZZPL).

I don’t use your data for advertising, I don’t sell it, and I don’t share it with anyone other than the service providers listed below.

## Cookies

The site sets no cookies and uses no advertising services or tracking of individual visitors, which is why there is no cookie banner.

## Who else has access

I use service providers that process data on my behalf:

- **Cloudflare** (USA): hosting, form protection, visit statistics and email forwarding.
- **Google** (Gmail): the mailbox where inquiries and emails arrive.
- **Brevo** (France): sending my replies.

These providers may process data outside Serbia, in the European Union and the United States. Transfers go to countries with an adequate level of protection or rely on the safeguards those providers apply, such as standard contractual clauses.

## Data retention

I keep inquiries and correspondence while we’re in touch and for no longer than two years after the last message. If we work together, the data needed for the contract and payment is kept for as long as the law requires. Visit statistics are aggregated and contain nothing that identifies a visitor.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict its processing or to receive it in a portable format, and you can object to its processing. Send your request to [support@kovar95.dev](mailto:support@kovar95.dev) and I’ll reply without delay, within 30 days at the latest.

If you believe your data has been processed unlawfully, you can file a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, Belgrade, [poverenik.rs](https://www.poverenik.rs)).

## Changes

If the way I process data changes, I’ll update this page and its last-updated date.
